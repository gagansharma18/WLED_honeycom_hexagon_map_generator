Physical Wiring & Assembly Blueprint

Assembly Checklist:

  • Data In (DI): Starts at Hex #1, Vertex 0.
  • Data Out (DO) $\rightarrow$ Next Data In: Follow red interconnect arrows in numbered sequence.
  • Common Ground: Connect GND of ESP32 directly to power supply GND.
  • Power Injection: Red/Black pads on diagram indicate suggested 5V/GND injection nodes.
